Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in each plan?

The full product -- rate-confirmation reading, freight and accessorial invoicing, payment tracking, and short-pay detection. Plans differ by fleet size, not by withholding features.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 30-day trial starts at signup with no credit card required.

Is there a contract?

No. ChargeGuard is month-to-month with no long-term contract.

Is pricing really locked in?

Yes -- Founding Pricing locks in your rate for your first 12 months on ChargeGuard, even if public pricing changes later.

Do I need any special hardware or software to use ChargeGuard?

No, the core product works with manual entry and the driver app alone -- nothing else to install or connect.

What counts as a truck for fleet-size purposes?

The number of active power units/trucks your carrier operates, self-declared at signup.

What happens after 30 days?

You choose a plan based on your fleet size to continue. There's no automatic charge -- you confirm before any subscription begins.

What happens if my fleet grows into a larger tier's truck range?

Contact us to move to the right plan at your next renewal. There are no surprise mid-cycle charges.

How are the tiers decided?

Tiers are based on the truck count you declare at signup.

Why do free-time and accessorial rates differ from one broker or shipper to another, and how does ChargeGuard handle that?

Each broker or shipper's agreed terms are stored once and applied automatically to every future invoice with them, instead of using one generic rule for everyone.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. ChargeGuard is month-to-month -- you keep access through the period you've already paid for, and you're never cut off early. Cancellation timing for your next billing cycle is governed by our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
