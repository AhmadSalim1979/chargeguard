The Manual Burden of Detention Claims

Detention pay is often left uncollected because the process of documenting it is manual and time-consuming. Drivers and dispatchers are already stretched thin, making it easy to skip this step. Without immediate documentation, the evidence needed to file a claim is often lost or forgotten.

The Cost of Missing Detention Pay

Fewer than 50% of detention invoices are actually paid, according to the ATRI 2024 study. This means small carriers are leaving real money on the table—money that could be reinvested into the business or used to cover operational costs.

How ChargeGuard Changes the Game

ChargeGuard eliminates the manual work of detention claims. Drivers capture a photo of the rate confirmation, tap Start/Arrived in the app, and ChargeGuard automatically applies the broker’s or shipper’s terms to generate a documented claim. No more reconstructing detention time after the fact—just instant, accurate claims.

How does ChargeGuard differ from manual detention tracking?

ChargeGuard automates the entire process, from capturing detention time to generating a documented claim. Manual tracking relies on drivers or dispatchers to remember and record detention time, which is often overlooked or forgotten.

Is ChargeGuard compatible with my existing systems?

ChargeGuard works independently of your current systems. Drivers use the ChargeGuard app to capture detention time, and the platform generates claims based on the broker’s or shipper’s terms—no complex integrations required.
