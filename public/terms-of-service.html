2. Nature of Service

ChargeGuard reads the rate confirmation for each load, captures the timestamped evidence of detention and accessorial events at each stop, applies the terms that apply to that load, determines the contracted and earned revenue, and builds the complete freight and accessorial invoice ready for you to review and send. It tracks the invoice through payment, reconciliation, and short-pay/recovery status. The exact scope of service is defined by your selected plan (Starter, Small Fleet, Growth, Scale, or a custom Enterprise plan) and detailed in your signed Client Service Agreement.
