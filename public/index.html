The Problem

A Load Can Earn More Than the Number Somebody Remembers to Invoice

Between the rate confirmation and the money hitting your account, revenue leaks in a dozen small, ordinary ways -- not because anyone's dishonest, but because nobody has time to track every one of them by hand.

Terms nobody re-reads

The rate con says FSC, stop-off pay, and specific accessorial terms -- but by invoice time, nobody's checking the document again.

Detention, layover, TONU

Your driver waited past free time, got held overnight, or showed up to a cancelled pickup -- and it never made it onto an invoice.

Lumper and reimbursements

A driver pays a lumper out of pocket and the receipt sits in a text thread, never making it back onto the invoice.

Billing mistakes

A line gets missed, a rate gets mistyped, or an invoice goes out incomplete -- and nobody catches it until the money doesn't show up.

Short pays

The broker pays less than the invoice, without explanation -- and without someone watching for it, that gap just quietly disappears.

No one watching the total

Not every load has every charge -- but without a system tracking it end to end, you can't tell which ones you're missing until it's too late to collect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChargeGuard a TMS?

No. ChargeGuard doesn't dispatch loads or replace your TMS. It focuses on the money: making sure what a load earned is correctly identified, billed, and accounted for. Keep the tools you already use for dispatch.

What does ChargeGuard actually do?

ChargeGuard reads your rate confirmation to determine what a load should pay, captures what actually happened at each stop (including detention, layover, TONU, and lumper evidence), builds the complete freight and accessorial invoice, tracks what was paid, and flags what's still missing -- including short pays.

Do I need an ELD or TMS integration?

No. ChargeGuard works from an uploaded or photographed rate confirmation, the driver app, and manual entry -- nothing else to install or connect.

Does it just do detention claims, or the full invoice?

The full invoice. Contracted linehaul and FSC alongside every legitimate accessorial charge, on one documented invoice -- not a separate detention-only claim.

Can it catch a short pay?

Yes. When a broker pays less than the invoice, ChargeGuard detects the gap and opens a recovery case so it's tracked, not forgotten.

How much does ChargeGuard cost?

ChargeGuard operates on a tiered flat-rate monthly subscription model banded by fleet size, under Founding Pricing: $17/month for 1-5 trucks, $39/month for 6-15 trucks, $79/month for 16-50 trucks, and $149/month for 51-150 trucks. Fleets of 151+ trucks are custom/contact pricing. Founding Pricing locks in your rate for your first 12 months.

Is there a contract or commitment period?

No. ChargeGuard operates on a month-to-month basis with no long-term contracts. You can cancel at any time.
