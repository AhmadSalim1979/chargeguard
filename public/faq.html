Frequently Asked Questions About ChargeGuard

Answers on what ChargeGuard actually does -- from reading the rate confirmation to reconciled payment. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly.

What ChargeGuard Does

What does ChargeGuard do?

ChargeGuard reads your rate confirmation to determine what a load should pay, captures what actually happened at each stop, builds the complete freight and accessorial invoice, tracks payment, and flags what's still missing -- including short pays.

Is ChargeGuard a TMS?

No. ChargeGuard doesn't dispatch loads or manage your fleet. It focuses on the money: making sure what a load earned is correctly identified, billed, and accounted for. Keep the tools you already use to operate.

Who is ChargeGuard for?

Owner-operators and small-to-midsize US trucking carriers, roughly 1-150 trucks, who don't have a dedicated back-office team tracking revenue across every load and broker.

Rate Confirmations

How does ChargeGuard read a rate confirmation?

Upload it or photograph it, and ChargeGuard reads the contracted linehaul, fuel surcharge, mileage, stop-off pay, and the specific accessorial terms for that broker or shipper -- stored once, applied automatically to future loads with them.

Do I need any special hardware or software?

No. ChargeGuard works from an uploaded or photographed rate confirmation, the driver app, and manual entry -- nothing else to install or connect.

Driver Workflow

What does the driver have to do?

A driver taps Start and Stop from the ChargeGuard app at each stop, and can photograph lumper receipts or detention evidence right from the app. No app-store download -- it's added to the home screen from the phone's browser, with real push notifications for trip updates.

Does the driver see rates or invoice amounts?

No. Drivers see only their own assigned trips -- arrival, departure, and evidence capture. Rates and invoice amounts are an office-side view.

Detention & Accessorials

What types of accessorial charges can ChargeGuard bill?

Detention, layover, TONU (Truck Ordered Not Used), and lumper reimbursement, each calculated from that specific broker's own terms and backed by captured evidence.

Does ChargeGuard know each broker's actual detention terms?

Yes. Each broker or shipper's own free-time allowance and rates are stored once and applied automatically to every future load with them, instead of a generic default.

How does ChargeGuard establish accuracy for a charge that might be disputed?

By capturing timestamped evidence at each stop and applying the specific terms that apply to that load, producing a documented, defensible charge rather than a reconstructed guess.

Freight Invoicing

Does it create the full freight invoice, or only a detention claim?

The full invoice. Contracted freight revenue and every legitimate accessorial charge are combined into one documented invoice, not a separate detention-only claim.

Does ChargeGuard send invoices to my broker automatically?

Today, ChargeGuard prepares the completed invoice as soon as it's ready -- evidence attached, terms cited. You review it and confirm sending, and ChargeGuard emails it straight to your own inbox to forward. Automated direct-to-broker sending is a separate capability in controlled testing, not yet available on every account.

How much invoice history can I see?

Reports cover a rolling 3-year window, so you can review what's been billed, paid, and reconciled well beyond a single year.

Payments & Short Pays

What happens if the broker pays less than the invoice?

ChargeGuard detects the short pay and opens a tracked recovery case, so the gap is surfaced instead of silently disappearing.

Can ChargeGuard handle partial payments?

Yes. Payments can be recorded manually or imported from a bank or factoring company's remittance CSV. Partial payments and known factoring/quick-pay deductions are tracked correctly against what's still outstanding.

Does ChargeGuard connect directly to my bank?

Not as a live bank feed today. You import a bank or factoring remittance CSV, or record payments manually, and ChargeGuard matches them to invoices automatically where it can.

Tax

Does ChargeGuard calculate tax on invoices?

Where supported by ChargeGuard's stored, effective-dated jurisdiction rules, yes -- tax is determined automatically and applied to the invoice. If a narrow scenario is unresolved, ChargeGuard blocks that specific tax component rather than guessing.

Does ChargeGuard file or remit sales tax?

No. Where supported by ChargeGuard's stored, effective-dated jurisdiction rules, tax is determined automatically on the invoice. If a narrow scenario is unresolved, ChargeGuard blocks that specific tax component rather than guessing. ChargeGuard does not file or remit tax on a carrier's behalf.

Data & Security

How does ChargeGuard maintain data confidentiality and isolation?

ChargeGuard maintains strict data isolation to ensure your information is kept confidential and separate from other carriers' data.
