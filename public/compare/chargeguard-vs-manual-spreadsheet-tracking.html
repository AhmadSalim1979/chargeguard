ChargeGuard vs. Spreadsheet Detention Tracking: How Automation Stacks Up

ChargeGuard automates detention and accessorial charge recovery, while manual spreadsheet tracking relies on time-consuming, error-prone processes. Here’s how they compare.

Published 2026-09-02

Time and Effort

ChargeGuard automates the entire process: drivers capture photos of rate confirmations, tap timestamps in the app, and the system generates claims. Spreadsheet tracking requires manual entry of every stop, arrival, and departure time, which is slow and prone to human error.

Accuracy and Compliance

ChargeGuard applies each broker or shipper’s specific contract terms automatically, ensuring accurate claims. Spreadsheets often miss or misapply terms, leading to underbilling or disputes.

Claim Generation and Tracking

ChargeGuard generates submission-ready claims with photo evidence and tracks them through draft, sent, paid, and disputed statuses. Spreadsheets require manual claim creation and lack built-in tracking.

Scalability

ChargeGuard scales effortlessly with fleet size, handling unlimited claims within each subscription tier. Spreadsheets become unwieldy as fleets grow, requiring constant updates and maintenance.

Cost

ChargeGuard’s flat-rate subscription is predictable, with no per-claim costs. Spreadsheets may seem free, but the time spent tracking and recovering charges can cost carriers far more in lost productivity.
