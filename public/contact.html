Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I contact ChargeGuard?

Our team will respond to your inquiry and provide information tailored to your fleet's needs. We'll guide you through the next steps to get started with ChargeGuard.

What information should I have ready when I reach out?

Having details about your fleet size, how you currently invoice and track payment, and any specific challenges you face will help us provide the most relevant information.

How does ChargeGuard handle my data?

ChargeGuard is designed to work with data you already have, such as manual timestamps or uploaded documentation. We prioritize data security and privacy in all our processes.

Do I need to change my existing systems to use ChargeGuard?

No -- ChargeGuard works with various data sources today, including manual entry and the driver app, regardless of what else your carrier already uses.
