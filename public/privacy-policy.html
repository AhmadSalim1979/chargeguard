Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026 • Qiyadon

1. Information We Collect

ChargeGuard processes freight revenue and billing data on behalf of small-to-midsize US trucking carriers -- specifically rate confirmation terms, load arrival/departure timestamps, detention and accessorial evidence, invoices and invoice versions, tax determination data, receivables, payment and remittance records, reconciliation information, and recovery-case records, provided by you via manual entry, the driver app, an uploaded document or photo (such as a rate confirmation or lumper receipt), or an imported bank or factoring remittance file. We collect only what is necessary to determine what a load should pay, build the invoice, and track it through to payment.

We do not collect personal information beyond what is required to configure and operate the service, and we do not sell, rent, or share your load, revenue, invoice, or payment data with third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Read and store rate confirmation and contract terms for each broker or shipper
  • Capture and record arrival/departure timestamps and accessorial evidence for each load
  • Determine contracted and earned revenue, including applicable tax, for each load
  • Build the complete freight and accessorial invoice
  • Track invoices through payment, reconciliation, and short-pay/recovery status
  • Communicate operational updates regarding your account
  • Improve the accuracy and reliability of the service

3. Data Retention

Load, revenue, invoice, and payment data is retained for the duration of your active engagement with Qiyadon. Upon termination, this data is deleted from our active systems within 30 days, subject to any legal or regulatory retention obligations.

4. Data Security

We maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your load, revenue, invoice, and payment data against unauthorized access or disclosure. Access is restricted to authorized personnel operating under confidentiality obligations.

5. Client Data Isolation

Each carrier's load, revenue, invoice, and payment data is stored in isolated environments. Your data is never accessible to other clients.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party infrastructure or communication tools in the delivery of the service. These providers are bound by data processing agreements that restrict their use of your data to the delivery of Qiyadon's services only.

7. Cookies

We use minimal technical cookies necessary for the operation of this website. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your Rights

You retain full ownership and control of your load, revenue, invoice, and payment data at all times. You may request deletion of your data or export of your records at any time by emailing contact@chargeguard.net.

9. Children's Privacy

ChargeGuard's services are not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to the primary contact on your account at least 14 days prior to taking effect.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact: contact@chargeguard.net
