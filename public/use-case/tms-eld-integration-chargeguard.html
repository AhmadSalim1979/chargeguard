ChargeGuard Works Without a TMS or ELD

ChargeGuard’s core detention and accessorial charge tracking works today via manual entry or CSV upload, independent of your TMS/ELD system. A direct TMS/ELD data integration is planned for a future release, not available today.

Published 2026-09-02, corrected 2026-09-02

Core Functionality Without a TMS/ELD

ChargeGuard’s core functionality—tracking detention and accessorial charges—does not require a TMS or ELD. You can manually enter data or upload a CSV file to start recovering charges immediately.

TMS/ELD Integration: Planned, Not Yet Available

A direct TMS or ELD data integration is on ChargeGuard's roadmap but has not shipped yet. Today, every carrier -- with or without a TMS/ELD system -- uses manual entry or CSV upload. Pricing for the integration will be announced when it's available.

Flexibility for Small-to-Midsize Carriers

ChargeGuard is designed for small-to-midsize carriers who may not have a TMS/ELD system or prefer manual entry. The platform adapts to your workflow, not the other way around.

No Lock-In to a Specific TMS/ELD

ChargeGuard does not require or favor any specific TMS/ELD system. Manual entry and CSV upload work the same way regardless of what system you use -- and a future integration is planned to stay optional, never a requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a TMS or ELD to use ChargeGuard?

No, ChargeGuard’s core functionality works without a TMS or ELD. You can manually enter data or upload a CSV file to start tracking detention and accessorial charges.

What if I already have a TMS or ELD system?

A direct TMS/ELD data integration is planned for a future release and is not available yet. Until then, every carrier -- including those with an existing TMS or ELD -- uses ChargeGuard via manual entry or CSV upload; there is nothing to connect or configure today.

Is ChargeGuard compatible with my existing TMS/ELD?

No TMS/ELD integration exists yet, so compatibility with any specific system hasn't been built or verified. Manual entry and CSV upload work the same way regardless of what TMS or ELD you use (or don't use) today.

Will I be able to switch to a TMS/ELD integration later?

When a TMS/ELD integration ships, it's planned as an optional add-on -- manual entry and CSV upload will keep working alongside it. There is no integration available to switch to yet.
