There is no separate hardware or software to install, and nothing to connect or configure. Every carrier uses ChargeGuard the same way, on day one.

ChargeGuard is designed for small-to-midsize carriers who want a simple way to start, regardless of what other software they run. The platform adapts to your workflow, not the other way around.
