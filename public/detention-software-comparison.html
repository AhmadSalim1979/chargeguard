What About Enterprise-Tier Platforms?

Enterprise-tier detention and accessorial data platforms are a different category entirely: pricing typically starts around $5,000 per year and scales to $50,000 or more, with setup via TMS API integration that typically takes several weeks and IT support. That is built for large carriers running dozens to hundreds of trucks with dedicated IT resources — not the self-serve, owner-operator-to-150-truck segment ChargeGuard is built for.

Common Questions

Is ChargeGuard the cheapest option?

Not necessarily on price alone — some per-load capture apps are free or priced around $50/month. ChargeGuard’s plans start at $59/month for 5–15 trucks. The difference is what is included: your own actual per-broker and per-shipper terms stored and applied automatically, without requiring an ELD, sized for small-to-midsize fleets rather than a single-load workflow.

Do I need an ELD or TMS to use ChargeGuard?

No. ChargeGuard's driver app captures arrival and departure with a tap, so no ELD is required to get started -- uploaded documentation and manual/CSV entry work too. Optional ELD/TMS integration is available as a paid add-on.

How is ChargeGuard different from per-load capture apps?

Per-load capture apps are solid tools for logging individual detention claims. ChargeGuard’s core difference is that it stores each broker or shipper’s own agreed free-time and rate once, and applies it automatically to every future claim with that partner — instead of you working out the applicable terms fresh each time.

What about larger, enterprise-tier platforms?

Enterprise platforms in this space are typically priced from $5,000 to $50,000+ per year with a multi-week TMS API integration. ChargeGuard is built for owner-operators and small-to-midsize fleets who want this capability without an enterprise IT project.
