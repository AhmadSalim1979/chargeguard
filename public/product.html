Revenue assurance, start to finish

Every Load, Followed From the Rate Con to Reconciled Payment

ChargeGuard is built around one question: did this load earn what it should have, and did you actually get paid for it? Below is the real lifecycle it tracks for every load -- what should happen, what actually happened, what got billed, and what's still open.

1. What Should This Load Pay?

Read Straight From the Rate Confirmation

Upload it or photograph it, and ChargeGuard reads the contracted linehaul, fuel surcharge, mileage, stop-off pay, and any other legitimate contractual components where supported -- plus the specific broker or shipper's accessorial terms. Stored once per broker, applied automatically to every future load with them, instead of a generic default.

2. What Actually Happened?

Captured as It Happens, Not Reconstructed Later

Your driver taps Start and Stop at each stop from the ChargeGuard app -- arrival, departure, proof of delivery. Detention evidence and lumper receipts get photographed right from the app. A dispatcher can also enter or adjust times by hand. Nothing waits until someone remembers it at the end of the week.

3. What Did the Load Earn?

Contracted Revenue, Plus Every Legitimate Extra

ChargeGuard combines the contracted amount with any detention, layover, TONU, or lumper reimbursement actually earned on the load -- each one backed by its own evidence and the broker's own terms, not a flat assumption.

Detention

Time waited past the broker's own free-time allowance, at that broker's own hourly rate.

Layover

Held overnight or an extended wait, billed at that broker's flat layover rate.

TONU

A scheduled pickup cancelled after dispatch, billed per that broker's own TONU terms.

Lumper

Third-party dock labor the driver paid for, reimbursed per the broker's policy, receipt attached.

4. Is It Ready to Bill?

Integrity Checks Before Anything Goes Out

Before an invoice is generated, ChargeGuard confirms the required documentation and terms are actually in place -- and where applicable, determines tax from stored, effective-dated jurisdiction rules, not a live lookup on every invoice. Anything genuinely missing is surfaced as an Action Needed item instead of silently skipped.

5. What Gets Invoiced?

A Complete Billing Packet, Ready to Review

Contracted freight revenue and every legitimate accessorial charge, combined into one documented invoice or billing packet -- evidence attached, the broker's own terms cited, tax applied where applicable. The moment it's ready, ChargeGuard delivers the complete packet to your inbox for review and sign-off. Direct-to-broker sending is in controlled rollout for select accounts.

6. What Did They Pay?

Receivable Tracking, Not Guesswork

Record a payment by hand, or import your bank's or factoring company's remittance CSV -- ChargeGuard matches payments to invoices automatically where it can. Multiple and partial payments are tracked correctly, including known factoring or quick-pay deductions, so the outstanding balance is always accurate.

7. What's Missing?

Short Pays Get Surfaced, Not Lost

When a broker pays less than the invoice, ChargeGuard detects the gap and opens a recovery case -- tracked through evidence, follow-up, and resolution, instead of quietly disappearing into an unreconciled balance.

8. When Is the Load Financially Done?

Reconciled and Closed -- for Real

A load isn't done when the invoice goes out. ChargeGuard tracks it through to reconciled and closed, so you always know which loads are actually finished and which still have money outstanding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ChargeGuard replace my TMS?

No. ChargeGuard doesn't dispatch loads, manage drivers, or replace the tools you already use to operate. It focuses specifically on the money: what a load should pay, what it actually earned, what got billed, and what's still outstanding.

How does ChargeGuard know what a load should pay?

You upload or photograph the rate confirmation, and ChargeGuard reads the contracted linehaul, fuel surcharge, mileage, stop-off pay, and the specific accessorial terms for that broker or shipper.

What counts as the load's earned revenue?

Contracted revenue plus any legitimate additional charges actually earned on that load -- detention, layover, TONU, or lumper reimbursement -- backed by the evidence captured during the trip.

Does ChargeGuard determine tax on the invoice?

Where supported by ChargeGuard's stored, effective-dated jurisdiction rules, tax is determined automatically -- a deterministic lookup, not a live tax-research call on every invoice. If a narrow scenario is unresolved, ChargeGuard blocks that specific tax component rather than guessing.

Can ChargeGuard track partial payments and short pays?

Yes. Payments can be recorded manually or imported from a bank or factoring CSV, matched to invoices automatically where possible, and a short pay opens a tracked recovery case rather than silently disappearing.

Does ChargeGuard contact my broker or shipper directly?

ChargeGuard prepares the complete invoice or billing packet as soon as it's ready -- evidence attached, terms cited -- so you have a finished package to review and sign off on. Today, you confirm it and ChargeGuard delivers it to your own inbox to send. Direct-to-broker automated sending is a separate capability in controlled testing, not yet available to every account.

How much history can I see?

Reports cover a rolling 3-year window, so you can review what's been recovered, billed, and reconciled well beyond a single year.

What happens if I need to cancel?

If you cancel, you keep access through the period you've already paid for. Cancellation timing for your next billing cycle is governed by our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
